What is this?

Disputes Map (name pending) is a mapping project by schmilly (schmilly.xyz) to map out strikes, industrial actions, lock outs and other activities across so-called Australia, with potential future plans for more generally showing workers struggle and activism across Australia. The idea is to show people where and how workers are organising across the country and show that even locally it is happening.

Where do you get your information from?

Information is mostly sourced from disputes report and FWC ballots, as well as supplemental information from other sources when needed. That being said not all dispute reports were used.

I contacted (via Email) Dispute Report/Sarah Missen for permission and to check they're okay with their work being injested and shown in the way it is shown on this page. I have yet to hear back.

How is the data processed?

Each dispute report and a collection of FWC filings is ingested into a JSON format via Deepseek. each entry is hand checked afterwards and also cleaned up and improved. AI is also unable to track long term strikes and disputes so adding ID’s is all done manually, and additional sources as well as number of workers affected is added in manually.
I am hoping to one day not use AI at all for this project, but instead rely on collaboration and hand crafted entries.

How is this hosted?

This is hosted on a laptop in my living room and pushed to the internet via a Cloudflare tunnel. This may cause down time and access issues.
Can also be accessed at schmilly.github.io/Dispute Map/
